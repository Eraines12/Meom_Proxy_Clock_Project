About this project

MindDial AI builds on a ResNet50 research baseline and its four drawing-feature criteria: Closed Circle, All 12 Numbers, Correct Number Position, and Correct Hands. It is an educational/research prototype and does not diagnose any medical condition.

Age and education do not change scores. Demographic subgroup performance has not been evaluated.
